Plan a responsive homepage for Orbit, a fictional note-taking app. Keep the layout simple and accessible.

Nov 6, 2026 at 07:37 pm

Homepage plan

  1. Hero: one clear headline, a short explanation and a primary action.
  2. Features: three cards describing quick capture, organization and search.
  3. How it works: create a note, add a tag, find it later.
  4. Footer: support and privacy links.

Use a single column on phones and a three-column feature grid on wide screens. Keep headings in order and make every control reachable with a keyboard.
